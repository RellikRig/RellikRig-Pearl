The archive includes the notices recovered and matched so far. It is **not a complete dependency inventory or corresponding-source bundle**. It does not change the installers or replace any rights granted by the upstream licenses.

- **Linux AppImage and Ubuntu package:** Recovered the notices and originating revisions for the bundled Pearl/NOCK proof utilities. Read the dependency index inside the archive for those versions and upstream files. Exact build records for two separate bundled mining engines and the full bundled library notices are still unresolved.
